# Structured Book Reader Surface

## Scope and visitor mode

- **Primary target:** `reader.html`
- **Mode:** Read.
- **Scope:** Make every existing chapter dossier readable, preserve the source/interpretation boundary, expose the two completed distillations, and move between chapters without filesystem search.

## Audience, job, and action

- **Audience:** Chief returning to a completed book and the explanatory work built from it.
- **Job:** Choose a book and chapter, read the source-grounded dossier, deliberately enter a constructive distillation when one exists, and retain provenance throughout.
- **Primary action:** Select and read a chapter dossier.
- **Secondary action:** Switch to a completed distillation or continue to the adjacent chapter.

## Proof and content

- 23 canonical chapter dossiers compiled from the global Book Schematics memory root.
- 2 completed constructive distillations: AI 2041 Chapter 1 and Jung Chapter 1.
- Explicit source ranges, layer status, reading measure, evidence grammar, and canonical path per document.
- Jung chapter maps remain labeled as initial structural passes.
- Unfinished distillations remain visible as `not yet developed` rather than fabricated.

## Constraints

- Editorial / magazine primary; refined-minimal seasoning.
- Lenis `2.0` on the reading plane only; native chapter rail; trail off.
- Immediate entry: no repeated aperture or prose reveal system.
- Static HTML contains all documents before JavaScript enhancement.
- Complete reduced-motion, library-failure, no-JavaScript, and offline states.
- No editor, search dashboard, annotation system, progress gamification, or PDF viewer.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

- **Direction:** The existing Living Schematic Folio becomes an annotated reading folio: Shippori Mincho article plane, Zen Kaku Gothic evidence, square layers, hairline chapter rail, and one muted-yellow volume language grounded in the *Modern Man* theme.
- **Memorable moment:** Selecting a chapter quietly replaces one paper leaf while the rail retains book, chapter, and epistemic layer.

## Unresolved decisions

- Archive-wide search and cross-book mechanism retrieval belong to a future index surface.
- Annotation and source-PDF viewing are intentionally outside this Reader.
