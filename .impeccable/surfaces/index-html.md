# Structured Book Shelf Landing Surface

## Scope and visitor mode

- **Primary target:** `index.html`
- **Mode:** Persuade through evidence, with a Read-quality long-form experience.
- **Scope:** Orient the reader to the Structured Book Shelf, prove the archive through two real volumes, explain the source-to-distillation method, and provide one entry action.

## Audience, job, and action

- **Audience:** Chief returning to or presenting a long-arc personal research archive.
- **Job:** Understand why the shelf is more than a summary collection and recover the path from book to source-grounded working model.
- **Primary action:** `Enter the shelf` → AI 2041, the first real volume.
- **Secondary action:** Inspect the representation method.

## Proof and content

- Verified archive inventory: 2 books, 23 chapter maps, 31 Markdown artifacts, 2 distillation pilots as of 2026-08-20.
- Real AI 2041 and Jung representation grammars, excerpts, page ranges, and canonical paths.
- Original schematic-edition cover studies, explicitly labeled as not the source jackets.
- No commercial proof, testimonials, users, prices, or completion claims may be invented.

## Constraints

- Source and interpretation remain visibly separate.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS; standalone works offline over `file://`.
- Local vendored fonts and motion libraries only.
- Complete reduced-motion, no-motion, and JavaScript-failure states.
- Lenis `2.0`; trail line off.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

- **Direction:** Editorial / magazine primary, refined minimal seasoning, grounded in Stripe Press’s book-object presence and color-field pacing but translated through Japanese restraint: Shippori Mincho, Zen Kaku Gothic New, warm mineral paper, sumi-like ink, and the muted yellow inherited from the *Modern Man* volume.
- **Memorable moment:** One book-like aperture opens to reveal the complete promise and two authored schematic editions.

## Unresolved decisions

- A separate archive-index surface will be needed when locating a specific author, chapter, claim, or open question becomes more important than first-contact orientation.
- Direct in-browser reading of Markdown dossiers is deliberately outside this landing surface.
