# Structured Book Shelf — Representation Decision

**Status:** Approved direction; implementation contract  
**Direction:** Editorial / magazine primary, Luxury / refined minimal seasoning  
**Scroll:** House default, Lenis `2.0`  
**Trail line:** Off

## Representation analysis

### Raw situation

Two books have been transformed into a real, source-grounded archive of chapter maps, measured structures, epistemic labels, open questions, and constructive-distillation pilots. A reader needs to perceive why this archive matters, enter it without file-system search, and trust where source ends and interpretation begins.

### Current representation

The incumbent page represents the product as a continuous 3D shelf containing seven imagined field guides for software tools.

**Type:** Homomorphic. It preserves the tactile pleasure and selection operation of a physical shelf, but collapses the actual archive’s source structure, provenance, claim boundaries, and living research function.

**Operations made cheap:** visual browsing, selecting a physical volume, inspecting craft.  
**Operations made expensive:** understanding the real archive, comparing source and interpretation, recovering chapter structure, recognizing what is complete, and seeing how a book becomes a cognitive instrument.  
**Invariant exposed:** books are authored objects worth returning to.  
**Invariant hidden:** every interpretation must remain attached to recoverable evidence.

### Alternative 1 — Publisher catalogue

**Type:** Homomorphic.

- **Operations cheap:** orientation, admiration, title-level browsing, editorial reading.
- **Operations expensive:** inspecting the transformation from source to schematic.
- **Invariant exposed:** each book has a distinct intellectual and material identity.
- **Invariant hidden:** chapter-level evidence and corrigibility.
- **Search reduced:** choosing a title becomes easy.
- **Transformation cost:** low, but it risks becoming a beautiful store with nothing to purchase.

### Alternative 2 — Archive dashboard

**Type:** Near-isomorphic index of files and statuses.

- **Operations cheap:** retrieval, filtering, counts, completeness checks.
- **Operations expensive:** grasping why the work matters and entering a book as a whole.
- **Invariant exposed:** inventory, status, provenance, and path structure.
- **Invariant hidden:** the felt intellectual transformation and the unity of each book.
- **Search reduced:** locating a file becomes easy.
- **Transformation cost:** moderate; high risk of generic knowledge-management chrome.

### Alternative 3 — Living schematic folio

**Type:** Hybrid representation: the book remains the primary authored object while its schematic layers become visible as a structured editorial folio.

- **Operations cheap:** understand the promise, inspect two real volumes, see the source-to-distillation mechanism, and enter by intellectual problem rather than filename.
- **Operations expensive:** exhaustive file management and rapid scanning of many future volumes.
- **Invariants exposed:** book unity, source recoverability, chapter structure, epistemic boundaries, and interpretive corrigibility.
- **Invariants hidden:** low-level file operations that do not improve first-contact understanding.
- **Search reduced:** the reader moves from book → governing question → mechanism → source path without browsing the raw archive.
- **Transformation cost:** higher editorial authorship and careful manual curation as the shelf grows.

## Recommendation

Build **Alternative 3: the living schematic folio**.

Use Stripe Press as evidence for full-field color chapters, oversized book objects, asymmetry, narrow text measures, hairline actions, and slow editorial pacing. Reject its commerce grammar, testimonial grids, publisher identity, and exact composition. Translate the reference into a private research instrument whose proof is the real archive.

## Search-reduction judgment

The folio reduces the search that matters at first contact: “What is this, what books exist, what changed in them, and can I trust the representation?” It deliberately does not optimize the later task “find any file instantly”; that belongs to a future archive index, not this landing surface.

## Product soul

> Structured Book Shelf turns finished books into source-grounded instruments for re-reading, explanation, inward examination, and responsible thought—without confusing the book with what we build from it.

## Seven-line craft brief

1. **Thesis:** The page behaves like opening a private scholarly press where every beautiful volume exposes the source structure beneath it.
2. **Signature gesture:** A single book-like aperture parts on entry, revealing the two-volume shelf; it plays once and never loops.
3. **Page entry:** Over approximately 1.25 seconds, the central hairline draws, the two veil leaves part, the wordmark recedes, and the complete hero is already legible beneath.
4. **Section transition:** One quiet parallax handoff lets the opening recede beneath the archive argument; the footer rises only through an inner wrapper so the terminal seam stays opaque.
5. **Scroll instrument:** Lenis house weight `2.0` governs wheel/trackpad; one parallax handoff and a desktop tick ridge reflect progress. Trail line is explicitly off.
6. **Hover grammar:** Every textual control uses the same upward label roll and hairline/ink attention shift; no glow, pill, bounce, or competing gesture.
7. **Rest state:** Warm mineral paper, sumi-like ink, one muted schematic-yellow accent, Shippori Mincho prose, and Zen Kaku Gothic evidence; authored book objects, visible provenance, square geometry, and enough silence to read without motion.

## Value spine

1. **Promise:** The shelf begins after the last page.
2. **Clarity:** Explain that a schematic preserves source, structure, mechanism, interpretation, and future correction.
3. **Proof:** Show the verified archive inventory and two real book objects before broad claims.
4. **Mechanism:** Demonstrate source → measured structure → chapter dossier → constructive distillation.
5. **Transfer:** Show how AI 2041 and Jung require different epistemic grammars while sharing one provenance discipline.
6. **Action:** Enter the shelf and move to either real volume or the method.

## Cuts

- The seven imagined software-tool books.
- Purchase-like actions, prices, testimonials, badges, and generic social proof.
- A full file browser, search box, dashboard metrics, and status-card grid.
- WebGL, audio, drag-to-open physics, and interaction complexity that delays understanding.
- Decorative “AI mind” imagery, wireframe motion, glow, and generic synthetic-intelligence theater.
- Any current-world claim inferred from a 2021 source without an explicit update boundary.

## Integration

- Physical-book craft + source-object provenance become one artifact.
- Book identity + chapter architecture remain visible without turning the page into a file tree.
- Interpretation + disagreement remain connected through explicit labels and source paths.
- Motion + reading become one pacing system rather than separate spectacle.

## Tradeoff

The folio is less playful than the incumbent 3D shelf and less operational than a dashboard. It gives up exhaustive browsing and simulated material physics to make the real archive, provenance boundary, and intellectual transformation immediately legible. As the collection grows beyond a small shelf, a separate index representation will become necessary rather than forcing the landing page to become a catalogue grid.

## Acceptance test

- Within five seconds, a first-time reader can say what Structured Book Shelf does and why it differs from a summary app.
- The first viewport contains the full promise, a real shelf artifact, and one dominant action without required scrolling.
- The page accurately shows 2 schematic roots, 23 chapter maps, 31 artifacts, and 2 pilot distillations as of 2026-08-20.
- AI 2041’s story/analysis/forecast boundary and Jung’s text/inference/meaning-frame boundary are visibly different.
- Source paths and the source-versus-interpretation rule remain recoverable.
- No section exists only to restate a claim; no proof is fabricated.
- Desktop and mobile reading remain coherent without hover or motion.
- The standalone works offline over `file://`, emits no console errors, and makes no network requests.

## Reader extension

The approved folio now hands its primary action to a distinct source-aware reading surface. The Reader makes all 23 chapter dossiers and the two completed distillations readable without adding long-form content to the landing sequence. Its representation, progressive static fallback, cut, tradeoff, and verification contract are recorded in [`READER-REPRESENTATION.md`](READER-REPRESENTATION.md).

## When to switch representations

Add a separate archive-index surface when the collection becomes large enough that locating a specific author, claim, unresolved question, or cross-book mechanism becomes the dominant job. Preserve the folio as the orientation and meaning layer and the Reader as the sustained chapter layer; do not turn either into the dashboard it intentionally rejects.
